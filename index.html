Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

MG Avenues Builders & Developers (“MG Avenues”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through this website may be collected, used, stored and shared when you request a brochure, schedule a site visit, enquire about a plot, or submit a plot booking application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect your name, mobile/WhatsApp number, email address, site-visit preferences, plot/project details and other information you voluntarily provide. Where a booking form asks for identity information such as Aadhaar or PAN details, those details are collected for the stated booking, verification or administrative purpose.

2. How We Use Information

We may use submitted information to respond to enquiries, send requested brochures, arrange site visits, communicate about plot availability and booking requests, verify submitted details, provide customer support, and maintain records relating to your enquiry or transaction.

3. WhatsApp and Communication

If you choose to contact or submit a request through WhatsApp, the information you provide may be transmitted through WhatsApp and used by MG Avenues to respond to your request. WhatsApp has its own privacy terms and policies, which apply to information processed by WhatsApp.

4. Data Sharing

We may share information with employees, authorised representatives, service providers or professional advisers where reasonably necessary to process your enquiry, booking, verification, communication or related service. We do not intend to sell personal information as a commercial mailing list.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through this website and retain information only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, legal or regulatory requirements, dispute resolution, or legitimate business records.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy-related concern, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification.

7. Contact Us
